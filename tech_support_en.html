Support

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Reminders are not firing on time

  • Check that the system granted notification permission: Settings → Notifications → Tallyon.
  • Turn off Focus / Do Not Disturb, or add this app to the allowed list.
  • Once today's goal is met, the app silences the remaining reminders for that day on purpose — that is by design.
  • Make sure the master Notifications switch (Me → Notifications) is on.

Data did not sync after switching devices

  • Sign in with the same Apple ID on both devices and turn on iCloud.
  • Trigger a manual sync: Me → iCloud Sync.
  • Sync is last-write-wins and does not merge conflicts. If the other end was written by a newer version and cannot be read here, your local data is kept untouched.

Restoring a subscription

  • Go to Me → Upgrade to Pro → Restore Purchases and use the same Apple ID. You will not be charged twice.
  • If no purchase is found, confirm you are signed in with the Apple ID used at the time of purchase.
  • To cancel renewal: iOS Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.

Home Screen widget / Lock Screen Live Activity missing

  • Widgets require iOS 16.2 or later. Long-press the Home Screen → ➕ → search for this app.
  • If it stays empty after adding, open the app once to refresh the data.

Importing or backing up data

A list you already wrote can be imported in one go from Me → Bulk import (one item per line, or a CSV / TSV exported from Excel or Numbers). Your data lives on this device and in your own iCloud, and is covered by the iPhone's system backup.
