Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

What We Collect

Nothing. Tallyon has no accounts and no server of its own. Your check-in records, lists and settings stay on your device — the developer cannot read them, and no backend ever sees them.

Where Your Data Lives

  • On this device: structured JSON inside the app sandbox (removed when you delete the app).
  • iCloud: if you turn sync on, data is written to your own private iCloud key-value store so it can follow your other devices. The developer has no access to it.
  • Apple Calendar: only when you explicitly run Calendar Sync are upcoming check-in days written to your Apple Calendar. You can turn it off and clean up at any time.

System Permissions We Request

  • Notifications: to remind you at the times you set. Turn it off at any time with the master Notifications switch.
  • Face ID / passcode: used only for the privacy lock. Verification happens entirely on device.
  • Calendar: used only for Calendar Sync.
  • Photos (add only): used only when saving an achievement card or wallpaper image.
  • Microphone: used only when recording a custom reminder sound, which is stored on device only.
  • Widgets / Live Activities: to show today's progress on the Home Screen and Lock Screen.

Third-Party SDKs

None. There is no analytics, sharing or push SDK in this app, so no data is shared with third parties. Every capability comes from Apple's own system frameworks.

Subscriptions and Payments

Subscriptions are handled by Apple's StoreKit. Payment details (such as your card) stay with Apple — we never receive them. Subscriptions renew automatically within 24 hours before the current period ends, and you can cancel any time in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes, the "last updated" date above is revised in a new version. Continuing to use the app means you accept the updated policy.
