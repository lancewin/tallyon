The Service

Tallyon gives you tools for habit check-ins, to-do lists and focus timing. The app is provided "as is" and makes no promise that you will reach any particular self-discipline goal.

Accounts

There is no sign-up, no login and no account system, so there is no account deletion or account recovery to speak of.

Free Tier and Pro

  • Free: up to 3 habits, with up to 3 reminders per habit per day.
  • Pro: unlimited habits, up to 10 reminders per day, and the privacy lock (Face ID / passcode).
  • Statistics reports, iCloud sync and all themes are available in the free tier as well — they do not require a subscription.
  • The price shown in the App Store applies (it may be in your local currency).

Subscription, Trial and Refunds

  • Pro is an auto-renewable subscription, billed yearly, with a 3-day free trial.
  • Cancel during the trial and you are not charged. Cancel during a period and it simply does not renew after that period.
  • The subscription renews automatically within 24 hours before the current period ends, charged by Apple to your Apple Account.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple — request one from your purchase history in the App Store.
  • You can restore a previous purchase inside the app with "Restore Purchases". It will not be charged twice.
  • To cancel: Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.
